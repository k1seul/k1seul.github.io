## Summary

This thesis compares an artificial agent (a Deep Q-Network) and a natural agent (rhesus monkey) on a 3D navigation task. It introduces a curiosity-driven maximum-entropy reinforcement learning model that combines a goal-based extrinsic reward with a curiosity-based intrinsic reward in a non-linear way.

## Task Design

Rhesus monkeys were trained on a 3D navigation task with multiple sub-rewards and a daily-changing jackpot location. The same environment was ported to a 2D pygame environment for training RL agents: DQN (with and without batch learning), model-based RL, and the proposed curiosity-based RL.
